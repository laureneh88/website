Lemon & herb pasta

25 min · Serves 2
Ingredients
  • Pasta
  • Lemon
  • Olive oil
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Fresh basil
Method

Cook the pasta until just tender. Toss with olive oil and lemon, fold in the tomatoes, and finish with torn basil. Serve warm.
