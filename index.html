Lauren Hodge

Spotify 2019 - Present

Monetization & Expansion
  • Led the experimentation proving that add-ons wouldn't just grow revenue, it would protect it. Users who subscribed to add-ons churned at 1/3 the rate of single-product subscribers.
  • Built and shipped the add-on portfolio starting with Audiobooks+, driving 2M+ new subscribers at nearly 7x the lifetime value of base plan subscribers and establishing the foundation for a tiered product model.

Activation
  • TuneMyMusic integration: Reduced friction to new-user activation by enabling playlist transfers from other music services.
  • Personalized the free-to-paid journey by defining the signal layer (skip limits, feature usage, search behavior, engagement lapse) that identifies what each user values and delivers the right reason to upgrade at the right moment. Built the prioritization logic that prevents high-value touchpoints from being overused, and the attribution to measure what works.

Retention
  • Designed and shipped "Your Benefits," a Premium value surface that reduced active cancellations by making subscribers aware of what they would lose before they decided to leave.
  • Recovered involuntary churn by surfacing payment failure and expiration banners across key touchpoints before users lapsed.

Financial Engineering

Built the systems that calculate how much Spotify owes rights holders for every stream, handling thousands of unique deal structures across every currency and market Spotify operates in.
